Overview

Learning the 3D world, end to end.

End-to-End 3D Learning (E2E3D) brings together researchers in computer vision, robotics, graphics, and machine learning to advance unified models that map raw images, video, and sensor streams to geometry, semantics, and action.

The workshop focuses on reproducible methods, open assets, and reliable perception for robotics, autonomous driving, extended reality, and scientific imaging.

Modeling and learning

Geometric foundation models, world models, vision-language-action systems, differentiable rendering, and physics.

Data and pretraining

Large-scale video and multi-view data, cross-modal alignment, automatic annotation, and responsible data curation.

Systems and evaluation

Accuracy, latency, memory, energy, robustness, and deployment in real-world environments.
